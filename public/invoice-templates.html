Tips
Invoice number is auto-generated. Never include it in the paste — the system assigns the next available number after you click Generate.
Route separator: Always use KL - KLIA (space-hyphen-space). Arrows like → or the word "to" also work but hyphens are safest.
Date anywhere works: Put date on the invoice header line OR per trip row. If a row has no date, the header date is used.
Free-form text is fine. The AI reads natural language — you can paste a WhatsApp message as-is and it will extract the data.
No Chinese characters. They are stripped during PDF generation. Write routes in English only.
No slash in routes. KL - KLIA / airport → everything after / is cut. Keep routes clean.
